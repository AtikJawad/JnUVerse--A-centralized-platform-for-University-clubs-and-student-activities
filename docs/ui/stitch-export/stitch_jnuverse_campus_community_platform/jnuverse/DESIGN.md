## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, high-end digital ecosystem for Jagannath University’s academic, social, and student community. Blending contemporary academic prestige with progressive modernism, the experience breaks away from bureaucratic, cluttered institutional portals. The mood is sophisticated, open, and forward-looking—evoking intellectual ambition, community pride, and effortless digital fluidity.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tonal Layering**:
- **Bespoke Asymmetry:** Editorial grid compositions using asymmetrical balance, oversized typographic scale, and elevated negative space.
- **Surface Articulation:** Pure white elevated cards resting over whisper-soft periwinkle and lavender tinted backdrops, defined by crisp hairlines rather than heavy fills.
- **Restraint & Precision:** Tactile pill micro-elements, high-contrast typography, and purposeful primary indigo interactions, devoid of muddy blurs or neon gradients.

## Colors

The palette balances airy, ethereal pastels with commanding academic deep indigo to ensure sharp visual contrast and AA/AAA accessibility.

- **Primary (`#2B34B9`):** Reserved for core visual anchors, key call-to-actions, active navigation markers, and brand hallmarks. On interactive states, transition directly into `primary-hover` (`#1F2792`).
- **Secondary (`#AEACF9`):** Accent periwinkle used for featured badges, active community tabs, indicator rings, and subtle tonal washes.
- **Tertiary (`#B2C7F7`):** Powder blue supporting secondary tags, secondary event markers, and illustration fills.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:** 
  - `canvas-base` (`#F8F8FF`) sets an atmospheric, cool lavender-white foundation.
  - `surface-white` (`#FFFFFF`) lifts functional interactive containers from the canvas.
  - `surface-lavender` (`#EBECF9`) provides soft section demarcation and nested card backings.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E4F3`) provides crisp 1px borders to preserve structural order without visual weight.
- **Typography Tone:** Headings and primary copy command attention in `neutral` deep slate (`#131526`), while descriptions, metadata, and timestamps utilize `text-muted` (`#686B7D`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is driven by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, rounded welcoming counters, and editorial presence.

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings feature tightened negative letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and structured line-heights to produce an authoritative, confident editorial statement. Use bold and extra-bold weights to anchor section intros and hero moments.
- **Body:** Standard body text prioritizes legibility with generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.65` ratio) and balanced character rendering to prevent eye fatigue during long community articles and academic notices.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Badges, tags, and navigation items leverage medium-to-bold weights with slight positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.04em`), ensuring instant scannability on small surfaces and status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an asymmetrical 12-column responsive grid on desktop, scaling cleanly to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (≥1200px): Max-width `1360px`, outer margins `4rem`, column gutters `2rem`.
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): Fluid, outer margins `2.5rem`, column gutters `1.5rem`.
  - Mobile (<768px): Fluid, outer margins `1.25rem`, column gutters `1rem`.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Space out hero sections and community discovery galleries using expansive spacing increments (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish an open, editorial feel. Within card clusters and modular widgets, maintain tight cohesion using `space-sm` and `space-md`.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature spotlights (such as campus event showcases or guild features) should span asymmetric column spans (e.g., 7 columns for media/context paired with 5 columns for active metadata cards).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces and ultra-diffused atmospheric drops, rejecting aggressive glassmorphism and heavy borders.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Canvas):* `#F8F8FF` creates ambient atmospheric grounding.
  - *Tier 1 (Layered Backgrounds):* `#EBECF9` defines section containers, sidebar modules, or filter bars.
  - *Tier 2 (Elevated Content):* `#FFFFFF` provides pure white card faces and sheets.
- **Outlines & Borders:** All elevated cards and interactive inputs feature a delicate 1px border (`#E2E4F3`).
- **Shadow Tokens:**
  - *Resting Cards / Badges:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 52, 185, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(19, 21, 38, 0.03)`
  - *Floating Preview / Popover:* `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(43, 52, 185, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(19, 21, 38, 0.02)`
  - *Interactive Hover Elevation:* Elevate Y-axis by `-2px` to `-4px` while expanding shadow diffusion: `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(43, 52, 185, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a refined, modern roundedness. Geometry balances structured stability with organic softness:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, utility dropdowns, and compact tooltips.
- **Medium Radius (1rem / 16px):** Standard for embedded media thumbnails, nested elements, and secondary panels.
- **Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Core cards, community hubs, discovery modules, and floating modal panels.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** All action buttons, status indicators, department badges, filter pills, and navigation chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`9999px`), solid `#2B34B9` background, white bold text, with subtle shadow. Transitions to `#1F2792` on hover with a `transform: translateY(-1px)` lift.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Full pill, `#EBECF9` background, `#2B34B9` text. On hover, background shifts to `#AEACF9` with 30% alpha.
- **Editorial Outline:** Full pill, transparent background, 1.5px border in `#2B34B9` or `#E2E4F3`, text in `#131526`.

### Chips & Status Tags
- **Category & Department Pills:** Full pill, height `28px` to `32px`, padding `0 12px`. Light pastel backgrounds (`#EBECF9` or powder blue tinted) paired with bold micro-copy (`label-sm`).
- **Live / Event Badges:** Includes a 6px circular status dot (solid `#2B34B9` or green indicator) alongside `label-sm` text.

### Discovery & Preview Cards
- **Club & Community Cards:** Constructed with pure white surface, 1px `#E2E4F3` stroke, `1.5rem` corner radius, and resting ambient shadow. Inner layout features an asymmetric header, pill category tags, member avatar clusters, and an action link.
- **Event Snapshot Card:** Features full-bleed curved photography headers, date badges overlaid via floating white pill containers, and metadata rows using `body-sm`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, `0.5rem` radius, white surface, 1px `#E2E4F3` border, typography `body-md`. Focus state shifts border to `#2B34B9` with a soft 3px box-shadow ring in `rgba(43, 52, 185, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` custom controls with `#E2E4F3` border; checked state fills with `#2B34B9` and crisp white check/dot icon.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Feed Elements:** Separated by 1px subtle divider lines (`#E2E4F3`). Hovering an item reveals a whisper wash of `#EBECF9` with a 0.75rem rounded highlight effect.
